UPLOAD TO YOUR WEBSITE (FTP FROM PHONE)
=======================================

YOU NEED
  Folder:  deploy/website/   (from repo download or unzipped sudoku-studio-upload.zip)

STEPS
  1. FTP app → connect to your host.
  2. Open your web folder (public_html / www / htdocs).
  3. Delete OLD site files first if updating (especially old assets/*.js).
  4. Upload EVERYTHING inside  website :
       index.html
       assets/   (both files inside — required)
       favicon.svg, icons.svg, pwa-192.png, pwa-512.png
       manifest.webmanifest, registerSW.js, sw.js, workbox-*.js
       .htaccess
  5. index.html must sit directly in the web folder, next to  assets .

WRONG (do not upload)
  deploy/documentation/
  deploy/*.txt
  src/  docs/  from repo root
